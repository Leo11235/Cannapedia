# Cannapedia

Cannapedia is a free, Wikipedia-style encyclopedia dedicated entirely to cannabis: its archaeology and deep history, its chemistry and medicine, the decades of law and policy that shaped it, the culture that grew up around it, and the people who pushed the plant forward, from ancient steppe burial rites to the founders of modern hashish-making.

Every article aims to read like it was written by someone who actually knows the subject: specific, sourced, and free of the vague, evasive language that plagues so much writing about cannabis. Each entry carries an infobox of quick facts, cited references, and sidebar cards, similar in spirit to the ones you'd find on Wikipedia, surfacing trivia, timelines, quotes, and links to related articles.

## What's covered so far

Cannapedia currently includes a sweeping history of cannabis across twelve thousand years of human use, alongside biographies of some of the field's most influential figures: Frenchy Cannoli, the hashishin who helped revive traditional hand-made hashish in the modern American cannabis industry; Mila the Hashqueen, inventor of the machines behind modern mechanical and ice-water hash-making; and Sam the Skunkman, the breeder behind Skunk #1, whose genetics run through most of today's hybrid strains.

The encyclopedia is a living project and keeps growing, new articles, categories, and improvements arrive regularly.

## Philosophy

Cannapedia is built the way an encyclopedia should be: content and presentation kept separate, so articles can be written, corrected, and expanded without ever touching how the site looks, and so the site itself can keep evolving without anyone having to rewrite a single article. It leans on no single company or paid platform to exist.

## Disclaimer

Cannapedia is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation. Nothing here is medical or legal advice, cannabis law varies by jurisdiction and changes often.
