---
title: "About Cannapedia"
---
Cannapedia is a demonstration encyclopedia project focused entirely on cannabis: its archaeology and history, botany and chemistry, medicine, law and policy, culture, and the people who have shaped the plant's story.

It is built in the spirit of Wikipedia, an open, citable, constantly-extendable reference work, but as a smaller, independently run project with no affiliation to Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation.

## How articles are written

Every Cannapedia article is written in plain Markdown with structured metadata (an infobox, categories, and numbered references) sitting on top. That keeps the underlying content portable: the same article files can be rendered as a static site (as today), imported into a database-backed wiki engine, or exported to other formats, without being rewritten.

## How the site is built

Cannapedia is generated by a small Python script (`build.py`) that reads the Markdown articles, renders them with Jinja2 templates, and writes plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There is no required build toolchain beyond Python itself, which makes the project easy to preview locally and easy to deploy to ordinary static web hosting.

## Disclaimer

Cannapedia is provided for educational and informational purposes. It is not medical or legal advice. Cannabis laws vary widely by country, state, and region, and change frequently &mdash; always check current local law before acting on anything described here.
